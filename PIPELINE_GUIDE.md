# TNT Construction RATS - Application Pipeline Guide

## Pipeline Stages (in order):
1. **Submitted** - Applicant has applied
2. **Document Verified** - HR verified documents
3. **Shortlisted** - Candidate meets requirements
4. **Written Exam** - Candidate takes written test
5. **Interview** - Panel interview
6. **Medical Check** - Health screening
7. **Selected** - Hired! 🎉
8. **Rejected** - Not selected

## How to Move Candidates:

### From Submitted → Shortlisted:
1. Go to **HR Dashboard** → **Review Applications**
2. Click **"Shortlist"** button on a candidate
3. Candidate moves to Shortlisted status

### From Shortlisted → Written Exam:
1. Go to **Applications** → **Pipeline Management**
2. Filter by "Shortlisted"
3. Click **"Move to Written Exam"** 

### From Written Exam → Interview:
1. Go to **Pipeline Management**
2. Filter by "Written Exam"
3. Click **"Move to Interview"**

### From Interview → Medical Check:
1. Filter by "Interview"
2. Click **"Medical Check"**

### From Medical Check → Selected:
1. Filter by "Medical Check"
2. Click **"Select Candidate"**

### Generate Offer Letter:
1. After selecting, click **"Offer Letter"**
2. Fill in salary and details
3. Generate PDF offer letter
